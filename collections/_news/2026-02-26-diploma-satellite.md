I made a small [satellite imaging tool](https://ee-agnesfcameron.projects.earthengine.app/view/air-pollution-imagery-explorer) for the CCI's diploma in creative computing that allowed students to map and compare different air pollution indicators over time. I also made them a little [treasure hunt](/assets/img/news_img/satellite-challenge.pdf), to get students thinking about the images.
