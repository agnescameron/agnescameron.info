I made two new synthesisers for the [Commission for New and Old Art's](https://the-commission.vercel.app/) staging of [*Bow Down*](https://www.universaledition.com/en/Works/Bow-Down/P0008027). The original piece is scored for two flutes, two oboes, and percussion -- the synths replaced one oboe and one flute, and were referred to as *the Machines*. I will write about this soon!
