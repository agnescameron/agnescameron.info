to go with a chicken adobo, rice and mustard greens, I made a beansprout salad that sat somewhere between [a thai fried egg salad](https://hot-thai-kitchen.com/fried-egg-salad/) and [korean beansprout namul](https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/sukjunamul-muchim).

blanched and refreshed the beansprouts, then made a dressing by hitting garlic, salt, sugar and a birds eye chilli in a mortar and pestle (important). I added lime, fish sauce and a bit of veg oil, then mixed into the sprouts with chopped spring onion, mint and coriander. was particularly good after having sat for 20min (initially very peppery from chilli).
