Hi, I'm Agnes ↝↜

I design, build and maintain software and hardware, sometimes alone but often with friends. I have a specific interest in collective knowledge and infrastructural systems. At present, these interests are directed toward malleable manufacturing software, research work with satellite imagery, and making new sounds.

I work as a specialist technician at UAL's Creative Computing Institute, and direct the community interest company Inflationary Assets. I am currently an n-Space fellow at Somerset House Studios; previous affiliations include Bellingcat, the Mozilla Foundation, Knowledge Futures Group, New INC, MIT Museum Studio and MIT Media Lab. I make synthesisers and perform with the Commission for New and Old Art, and I was a founding member of the research studio Foreign Objects.

I play oboe with the Peckham Chamber Orchestra, edit Wikipedia under the alias petsquirrel, and enjoy making soft sculptural costumes. [x] [...]

If you are an activist group or community organisation looking for technical support or advice* (this has included things like satellite imaging, web/server advice and making inflatables), I'm normally happy to volunteer my time.
